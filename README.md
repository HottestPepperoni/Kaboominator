# Kaboominator -> A Snowball & Water Bomb Launcher
A fun, all-season DIY project! This 3D-printable launcher is designed to fire snowballs in winter and soft foam or cotton water balls in summer. Built around a simple linear catapult mechanism, it combines easy-to-print parts with common hardware for maximum outdoor fun.

Features

Launches snowballs (up to Ø 46 mm) or soft water balls
Uses DN50 HT pipe as the inner barrel and DN75 HT pipe as the housing
Powered by rubber bands (up to 5 mm wide)
Simple trigger mechanism and sliding sled
Fully 3D-printable components for connectors, trigger, and internal parts


Bill of Materials

3D-printed parts (STL files included)
DN50 HT pipe (inner barrel)
DN75 HT pipe (outer housing)
Rubber bands (up to 5 mm wide)
M4 screws and nuts
Sheet metal screws
Small compression spring


How It Works
The launcher uses a linear catapult principle:

A sled inside the barrel is pulled back by rubber bands
The trigger locks the sled in place
Release the trigger → sled accelerates forward → snowball or water ball launches!


Assembly Instructions

Print all parts using the provided STL files
Prepare the pipes (DN50 and DN75 HT)
Install the sled and trigger mechanism
Attach rubber bands and secure with screws
Test the trigger and adjust tension as needed

Detailed step-by-step guide is included in the /docs folder.

Safety Notes

Do not aim at people or animals
Use only soft projectiles (snowballs or foam balls)
Wear eye protection during testing
